# Full app brief — **Photo & Video AI Studio**

*(Ready to paste into Create.xyz’s app-builder prompt — written in English as requested.)*

---

## 1) App name & elevator pitch

**App name:** Photo & Video AI Studio
**Elevator pitch:** A powerful, user-friendly mobile and web app for creators that combines pro-grade photo and video editing tools with advanced AI features and a conversational AI assistant. Users can perform one-tap enhancements, complex edits (background removal, style transfer, color-grading), automated video editing (smart cuts, captions, sound design), and get step-by-step help from an English-speaking AI assistant.

---

## 2) Target platforms & audience

* **Platforms:** Web app + Progressive Web App (mobile-friendly) + option to export Flutter project for native Android/iOS.
* **Primary users:** Social media creators, small studios, freelance photographers/videographers, marketers, and hobbyists.
* **Skill levels:** Beginner → Advanced (UI adapts: Quick Mode vs. Pro Mode).

---

## 3) Main goals & success metrics

* **Goals:** Fast AI-assisted edits; high-quality exports; increase user retention via templates & assistant; convert free users to paid pro for high-resolution exports and premium AI credits.
* **Key metrics:** Daily active users, edits per user, export completion rate, paid conversions, average session length, retention after 7/30 days.

---

## 4) Core features (detailed)

### A. Unified Workspace

* Project-based workspace: one project may contain multiple images and video clips.
* Non-destructive editing with version history and undo/redo.
* Auto-save to cloud project storage.

### B. Photo Editor (essential tools)

* Crop, rotate, flip, straighten.
* Exposure / contrast / highlights / shadows / saturation / vibrance.
* White balance and temperature.
* Tone curves and HSL controls.
* Sharpening, denoise, blur (Gaussian, radial).
* Healing brush, clone stamp.
* Layers & blending modes (normal, multiply, screen, overlay).
* Gradients & vignette.
* Presets / LUTs / One-tap enhance.
* Batch edit & batch export.

### C. Video Editor (essential tools)

* Timeline with drag-and-drop clips.
* Trim, split, ripple delete.
* Multi-track audio mixing.
* Transitions library (cut, fade, slide, wipe).
* Speed ramping, reverse playback.
* Color grading panel (LUT import/export).
* Stabilization (AI-powered).
* Picture-in-picture and overlays.
* Export presets (social sizes, bitrate options).

### D. Advanced AI Editing (flagship)

* **Background removal**: single-click foreground mask for photos & masked region in video frames (frame-aware).
* **Generative fill & object removal**: replace removed objects with context-aware content.
* **Style transfer**: apply painterly, cinematic, or custom style to images or video sequences (per-frame stylization with temporal smoothing).
* **Auto color-grading / LUT suggestions**: AI recommends LUTs based on mood keyword (e.g., “moody teal-orange”).
* **Smart cropping & composition suggestions**: AI suggests crops for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube thumbnails.
* **Semantic selection**: select sky, hair, clothing, background, etc.
* **Facial retouching**: skin smoothing, blemish removal, teeth whiten, eye enhancement (preserve identity).
* **Auto enhance**: single-tap quality boost for photos or video clips.

### E. Automated Video Tools

* **Auto cut & assemble**: upload raw clips and a target duration; AI creates a paced edit with transitions and music.
* **Auto captions & subtitles**: speech-to-text with edit UI, multi-language support.
* **Auto highlight reel**: detect best moments using motion / face / audio peaks.
* **Music & SFX suggestion**: AI chooses royalty-free tracks matching mood and pace.
* **Auto color match**: match color across multiple clips.

### F. AI Assistant (conversational, English)

* **Role:** editable assistant name (e.g., “Studio Assistant”).
* **Capabilities:** explain steps, generate edits based on natural-language instructions, create presets, suggest templates, write social captions, produce export settings for platforms.
* **Examples of what users can ask (assistant):**

  * “Remove the background from this portrait and apply a soft golden hour look.”
  * “Create a 30-second Instagram Reel with fast cuts and upbeat music from these clips.”
  * “Make this photo look like a film still from the 1970s — color grade and add grain.”
* **Interactive actions:** assistant can run edit operations directly (with user permission), propose changes, and preview before apply.

### G. Templates & Marketplace

* Built-in templates for reels, thumbnails, promo videos, story templates.
* Allow users to import/export templates (.json or project file).
* Community template marketplace (optional monetization).

### H. Export & Sharing

* Export resolutions from social sizes up to 4K (Pro feature).
* Export formats: JPG, PNG, HEIC, MP4, MOV, GIF, WebP.
* Export presets: Instagram Stories, TikTok, YouTube 1080p/4K, Twitter, LinkedIn.
* Direct share integrations: Instagram (share link), TikTok uploader, YouTube (via OAuth), Google Drive, Dropbox.

### I. Collaboration & Assets

* Shared project links with view/edit permissions.
* Asset library: fonts, stickers, overlays, stock images & clips (free & premium).
* User-uploaded fonts and brand kit (colors, logos).

### J. Onboarding & Tutorials

* Interactive onboarding (choose Quick Mode or Pro Mode).
* Guided tour with sample project.
* AI tutorial: ask assistant “teach me how to color grade”.

## 5) Screens & navigation (high-level)

1. **Welcome / Sign-in** (email, Google, Apple)
2. **Home / Projects**: recent projects, templates, create new project
3. **Upload / Import**: files, camera, cloud
4. **Editor Workspace**: left toolbar (tools), canvas/timeline center, right properties panel, top AI assistant chat button
5. **Assistant Modal/Panel**: chat + suggested actions (Apply, Preview, Undo)
6. **Export dialog**: presets, advanced settings, watermark toggle
7. **Settings**: account, subscription, AI credits, storage
8. **Admin Dashboard** (web-only): user metrics, template moderation, billing

---

## 6) Data & backend model (basic)

* **User:** id, name, email, plan, preferences, brand kit
* **Project:** id, owner, assets[], edits[], versions[]
* **Assets:** file metadata, thumbnails, storage path, usage
* **Templates:** metadata, preview, author, price/free
* **AI jobs:** job id, status, input refs, output refs, cost (credits)

**Storage:** S3-compatible (cloud storage) for assets + CDN for delivery.
**DB:** PostgreSQL for metadata + Redis for job queue.

---

## 7) AI & integrations

* **AI services (suggested):**

  * Image generation/processing: Stable Diffusion / custom vision models / provider APIs
  * Background removal / matting: dedicated API (or open-source models like MODNet)
  * Speech-to-text: Whisper or cloud STT for captions
  * Text-to-speech: cloud TTS for voice-over suggestions
  * Video processing: FFmpeg + frame-level AI models
* **Third-party services:** OAuth (Google, Apple), cloud storage (S3), payments (Stripe), analytics (Mixpanel or Segment), error tracking (Sentry).

---

## 8) Monetization & plans

* **Free tier:** basic editor tools, low-res exports, watermarked exports, limited AI credits, access to free templates.
* **Pro tier (subscription):** high-res exports, unlimited projects, premium templates, batch processing, priority AI processing.
* **Credits & microtransactions:** buy AI credits for heavy AI jobs (style transfer, generative fill, long video rendering).
* **Marketplace revenue share** for premium templates/assets.

---

## 9) Permissions & privacy

* Request minimal permissions (camera, storage) only when necessary.
* Clear consent for uploading user content to AI/third-party services.
* Privacy: user retains ownership of their content; option to delete account & assets.
* GDPR & CCPA compliance considerations.

---

## 10) Accessibility & localization

* Accessibility: keyboard navigation, screen reader support, large text UI mode, color contrast.
* Localization: UI support for major languages; assistant initially English (with future multi-language support).

---

## 11) Admin features & moderation

* Template moderation queue.
* Abuse detection for generated content (nudity, hateful symbols).
* Admin controls for disabling accounts, viewing logs (with privacy safeguards).

## 13) Example AI assistant prompts & expected behavior (for training the assistant)

* User: “Remove the background from this portrait and add a soft cinematic teal-orange grade.”
  Assistant: *Applies semantic selection → removes background → applies teal-orange LUT preview → shows before/after slider → asks to accept or tweak intensity.*

* User: “Make a 20-second TikTok using these 5 clips, fast-paced with upbeat pop music.”
  Assistant: *Runs auto-cut, times to 20s, selects high-energy moments, applies transitions and matched music, generates captions, previews final clip.*

* User: “Replace the sky in this photo with a dramatic sunset and blur the background.”
  Assistant: *Detects sky region, replaces with generated sunset (user can choose from 3 variants), adds depth-of-field blur to background layer.*
